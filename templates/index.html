Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers, or reach out if you need more help.

How do I access free study materials?
All free materials can be found in the Study Materials section. Simply click on the title and you can download the attached PDF or view the resource directly on the site.
How does the paid premium material work?
For premium content, you pay manually by using our provided UPI scanner. After you complete the transaction, the file will be accessible. Our system automatically processes your registration safely.
Who do I contact for admission inquiries?
Please use our Contact page to send a direct message, or email us at the address provided in our footer. Admissions are reviewed thoroughly on an annual basis!
Where are you located?
Friends Shishu Bikash Academy is centrally located and serves the community proudly. Contact our administration office directly to set up an in-person meeting.
